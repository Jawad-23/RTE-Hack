# Croptions

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Farm planners in hot, dry regions compare crops, cooling systems, solar capacity and estimated economics before investing. The demo focuses on Qatar. English and Arabic must offer the same planning workflow.

## Design direction
Preserve the committed Croptions prototype and `ui/theme.py`: calm sand surfaces, green actions, readable data and visible assumptions. Extend the existing interface rather than introduce another visual style. Charts must retain units, and simulation must never be presented as live sensor data.

## Accessibility
Use labelled native controls, readable contrast, responsive layouts, RTL Arabic and reduced-motion alternatives. Colour alone must not communicate suitability.

## Scope
Plan → Build → Operate simulation. The Croptions Kit is simulated: a phone sends simulated readings, labelled as such on every screen. Cameras, ESP32, learned controllers and live hardware remain roadmap only. Model/provider configuration and credentials are maintained separately by the teammate.
